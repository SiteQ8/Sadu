## Frameworks referenced

- The Cyber and Operational Resilience Framework (CORF) belongs to the Central Bank of Kuwait. Sadu reproduces its structure (identifiers, structural titles and page numbers) so the register can be navigated, and writes its own summaries. The official text is the CBK publication.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is published by ISO and IEC. Only Annex A control identifiers and this project's own short descriptions are included.
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 is published by the PCI Security Standards Council. Only requirement and requirement group identifiers and this project's own short descriptions are included.
- The Customer Security Controls Framework (CSCF) is published by Swift. Only control identifiers, control titles, objectives, principles, mandatory or advisory status and this project's own short descriptions are included.

The crosswalk between these frameworks is this project's own analysis and is not an official mapping by any of these bodies. Sadu is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Central Bank of Kuwait, ISO, IEC, PCI SSC or Swift.
